Support

FaceAR turns an iPhone with a TrueDepth (Face ID) camera into a facial motion-capture device for Autodesk Maya.

Requirements

  • iPhone with a TrueDepth camera (any Face ID model), iOS 15 or later
  • For streaming: a computer running Autodesk Maya with the FaceAR plug-in, on the same network as the phone (or reachable over USB / a configured remote connection)

Common questions

The app says "No face"

Point the front camera at a face at roughly arm's length, in reasonable light. Face tracking uses the TrueDepth camera, so it only works with the screen-side camera.

Maya cannot find the phone

Both devices must be on the same network, and the app needs the Local Network permission (iOS Settings → FaceAR → Local Network). The app's top panel shows the address and port to enter in Maya if discovery is blocked by the network — some routers isolate Wi-Fi clients.

USB connection

On Windows, Apple Devices (or iTunes) must be installed so the Apple Mobile Device Service is running. Plug in, unlock the phone, and tap Trust.

Where are my recordings?

Takes recorded on the phone are listed in the app and can be downloaded from Maya, or exported from the phone via the Files app.

Calibration

Record or stream after taking a neutral-face calibration in the app; the calibration travels with every take, so nothing is lost if you reprocess later.
