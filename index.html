FaceAR — for use with Autodesk Maya

FaceAR turns an iPhone with a TrueDepth (Face ID) camera into a professional facial mocap device: 52 ARKit blend shapes plus head and eye movement, streamed live to Maya or recorded as takes on the phone.

Capture

Up to 60 fps face tracking with a live preview and a neutral-face calibration that travels with every recording.

Stream

Live to the FaceAR plug-in for Maya over Wi-Fi or USB, with zero-setup discovery — or dial out to a studio machine over the internet.

Record

Record takes with or without a computer connected, download them into Maya later, and save the last few minutes after the fact.

Free. No ads, no accounts, no analytics — see the privacy policy.
