Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

FaceAR does not collect your data. This page explains exactly what the app does with the information it touches.

Camera and face data

FaceAR uses the TrueDepth (Face ID) camera and Apple's ARKit to measure facial expressions — 52 blend-shape values plus head and eye rotation — for character animation.

  • All processing happens on your device.
  • Face data is sent only to computers you choose to connect to (for example, your own computer running Autodesk Maya), over your own network or a connection you configure.
  • Recordings ("takes") are stored on your device, under your control, and can be deleted at any time from the app or the Files app.
  • No face data, imagery, or depth data is ever sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • ARKit face tracking does not use Face ID and cannot identify you; Apple's Face ID authentication data is never accessible to apps.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and makes no connections to developer-run servers. We never see your data, so we cannot sell, share, or lose it.

Local network access

The app asks for Local Network permission so that your computer can discover the phone (Bonjour) and receive the capture stream. This traffic stays between your devices.

Changes

If a future version of the app changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
